• The right model, every time. Power Modes no longer lock in the dictation model, language and prompt that happened to be selected when they were created, so dictation stops switching models depending on which app is in front. Every Power Mode setting now shows "Use global setting" unless you choose otherwise.
  • Parakeet keeps your last words. Stopping a dictation right after you finish speaking no longer cuts the end off, and switching between Whisper models loads the new one once instead of on every dictation.
  • AI enhancement, rebuilt. One Output setting decides what happens (Instant, Instant + Refine, or Enhanced); each dictation is enhanced with exactly the prompt, provider and model it started with; the timeout setting works; and when enhancement is skipped or fails, Zerm tells you why instead of silently pasting the raw text. Prompts can now use their own provider and model.
  • Transcribe File. Drop in audio or video files and get a full transcript with speaker identification, timestamps, renameable speakers, and export to TXT, Markdown, SRT, WebVTT or JSON. Open any audio file with Zerm from Finder.
  • Meetings has been removed and replaced by Transcribe File. Meeting recordings saved by earlier versions are permanently deleted when 2.8.6 first launches. Dictation recordings and History are not affected.
  • A wider, current model catalog. New on-device models: Parakeet Unified (English), Parakeet 110M for 8 GB Macs, and ivrit.ai Whisper models tuned for Hebrew. Outdated Whisper tiny, base and small models and Parakeet V2 are retired, and anyone using one is moved to its replacement. Recommended now picks the best English, multilingual and Hebrew models for your Mac.
  • Cloud models are up to date and filterable by provider: OpenAI gpt-transcribe, Gemini 3.5 Transcribe, AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro, Mistral Voxtral, and the new Gladia provider. Model settings such as Output Format and Dictionary terms are now sent the way each provider actually supports them, and custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints are verified when you save them.
  • Dashboard time saved and totals follow the range you select, and History has a copy button on every row.
  • Zerm is translated into Hebrew throughout, including the Dashboard, History and Models screens.
