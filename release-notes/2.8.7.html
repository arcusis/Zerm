• Clipboard History. Zerm now keeps a private, encrypted history of everything you copy, right on your Mac. Press ⇧⌘V to search it, preview any item and paste it into the app you're in. Filter by kind (text, images, links, files, colors, emails, code) or by the app it came from. Items are previewed properly: links with their title and image, Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music and SoundCloud links as media cards, files with thumbnails, colors with their values, and code with syntax highlighting. Text inside copied images is searchable.
  • Clipboard History does more than paste. It has favorites, pinning, Paste Next for pasting a sequence of items, Copy & Merge (press ⌘C twice to append), text transforms, and merging or splitting items. The main window has a full Clipboard History page, too.
  • You decide what's kept. Clipboard History has its own settings: how long each kind is kept, a storage limit, clearing on quit, restart, lock, sleep or at a set time, apps to exclude (password managers are suggested), a sound for each action, and whether links are fetched for previews. Passwords and other confidential items are never saved.
  • A natural Hebrew voice for Read Aloud. The new on-device Blue voice reads Hebrew and English aloud without an internet connection. Download it from Read Aloud settings.
  • Many more on-device models. The Whisper catalog is back to its full range, with English-only and quantized versions. Parakeet V2 and Redux are added, along with Moonshine for English and a Russian model. Each model shows who made it, its license and where it comes from. Downloads can be paused and resumed, even after quitting Zerm.
  • Faster on M5 Macs. Dictation and on-device AI use the Neural Accelerators in M5 chips.
  • Your language, every time. The language you choose in Settings is used everywhere, so Hebrew and Russian no longer come out as Arabic. With My languages, automatic detection only chooses between the languages you actually speak.
  • Better with AirPods and other Bluetooth headphones. Zerm records from another microphone when one is available, so your headphones keep full sound quality while you dictate. The start sound plays when your voice is actually being picked up. A push-to-talk dictation now always ends when you release the key, so the microphone is never left open.
  • Dictation starts and pastes faster, and many screens are clearer, including Dictation History, Read Aloud History, Settings, Transcribe File, Dictation Models and Permissions.
